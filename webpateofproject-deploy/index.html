The Challenge

01 THE PROBLEM

Delayed Diagnosis & Connectivity

Farmers in remote areas often struggle with internet connectivity. Cloud-only AI solutions fail when they are needed most—in the field. Additionally, accessing expert advice is expensive and slow, leading to crop loss before a solution is found.

02 THE SOLUTION

Edge AI & Offline Sync

PhytoLens moves the "Brain" to the device. By quantizing a MobileNetV2 model to TFLite, we enable 14ms inference time completely offline. A local SQLite database provides instant treatment plans, syncing with the cloud only when connectivity is restored.
